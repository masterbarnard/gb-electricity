Live GB electricity data, updated every few minutes

This page reads Great Britain's electricity system straight from the public feeds and redraws it as it changes: which fuels are generating and how much, what the country is actually drawing, which way each interconnector is flowing, how much carbon a unit of electricity carries this half hour, and how far system frequency has drifted from 50 Hz.

The numbers are the ones the UK electricity market settles on — Elexon's BM-metered outturn, NESO's carbon intensity, and Sheffield Solar's estimate of the rooftop generation the transmission system cannot see. Where two datasets disagree, the disagreement is shown rather than averaged away.

How each figure is derived, and where it can mislead you, is set out on Methodology. The data sources, the endpoints called and the licences behind them are on About.
